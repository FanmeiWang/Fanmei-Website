Fanmei Wang, PhD

Engaging and creative data analyst, and a Certified Human Resources Professional, currently working for the Government of Canada. Prior to this role, I accumulated over 15 years of experience teaching and conducting research in HRM at universities and world-renowned research institutions. In addition to my subject-matter expertise, I have taught both quantitative and qualitative methods at the graduate level. My research focuses on diversity and cultural sensitivity; ethnic and racial minority experiences in HRM fields; and related policies.

Currently, I bridge HR domain work with AI architecture and full‑stack delivery—designing data contracts and lakehouse ingestion, building Python/Flask apps and APIs, and shipping ML/NLP features that improve insights, policy and engagement.

In parallel to delivery, I keep a steady build‑and‑learn stream—rule-based and LLM-powered MVPs/PoCs, ML/DL studies on workforce and survey data, and cloud‑based Data & AI patterns exercised under real constraints. I document evaluation, privacy trade‑offs and explainability so prototypes can graduate into dependable tools. That is what I mean by research‑grade data, production‑grade AI; HRM is my home field, but the rigor travels.
