Fanmei Wang, PhD

Engaging and creative data analyst, and a Certified Human Resources Professional, currently working for the Government of Canada. Prior to this role, I accumulated over 15 years of experience teaching and conducting research in HRM at universities and world-renowned research institutions. In addition to my subject-matter expertise, I have taught both quantitative and qualitative methods at the graduate level. My research focuses on diversity and cultural sensitivity; ethnic and racial minority experiences in HRM fields; and related policies.

AI for HR, end to end: I bridge HR domain work with AI architecture and full‑stack delivery—designing data contracts and lakehouse ingestion, building Python/Flask apps and APIs, and shipping ML/NLP features that improve insights, policy and engagement.

Highlights: (1) Language Competency Evaluation & Training Tool (v2.3 and earlier) — a bilingual‑skills recommender that I built and maintain as the sole developer (Top‑4 finalist, 2025 Public Service Data & AI Challenge); (2) Python, YAML contract–driven ETL to automate the employee‑month data pipeline (ingest → transform → curated). Data‑quality reporting and policy‑gated releases are planned.

As the Brand Ambassador for CRA, I am honored to represent and promote our brand both internally and externally.
